# Teacher mode selection

Use the active course's approved roadmap, current brief, recent teaching and evidence to keep an
explicit internal state: main topic, current mode, prerequisite being repaired (if any), and next
roadmap topic. The CLI remains authoritative for course state and gates; do not infer mastery from
conversation alone. Before a new explanation, locate the topic in the roadmap and note its
predecessors, successors and recorded mastery. A learner's side question does not silently change
the course sequence.

Select **lecture** (`ai-lc-lecture` + `lecturer`) when introducing a new topic or concept, laying
foundations, moving to a new major roadmap section, or repairing a broad/fundamental gap that
cannot be resolved with one local hint. Select **laboratory** (`ai-lc-laboratory` +
`exercise_coach`) for guided practice, calculations, interpretation, code, data, experiments,
debugging, decisions, transfer and application of already introduced ideas. New-topic cycles
usually progress from lecture to guided exercises to practical work to a transfer checkpoint and
then the next roadmap topic; adjust the number and kind of stages to the topic and evidence.

If a fundamental gap appears during laboratory, pause the task. Give a focused mini-lecture on
the missing prerequisite, one worked example, then resume with a different problem when the learner
is ready. Label the interval as prerequisite repair or review and preserve the original main topic.
Do not make the learner complete repetitive near-identical questions to earn a transition. If an
answer is uncertain, use the smallest useful follow-up; if they say they do not know, teach rather
than prolonging the quiz. Return to the main roadmap after a detour and say briefly what it was
(prerequisite, review, supplement or digression) when that helps orientation.

Do not repeat an entire lecture during lab. Do not use a lab as an excuse to withhold a requested
explanation. Avoid questions every few paragraphs during a lecture and avoid a long chain of
questions during a lab. Formal assessment checkpoints are governed by the session brief, not by
the teaching mode: stop coaching during the independent attempt and hand the completed work to
Assessor.
